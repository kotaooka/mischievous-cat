## 🐾 How to play in 30 seconds

1. **Walk up to something and punch it!** Knock things off tables and shelves to rack up "damage"
2. **If the owner sees you, you're done.** A "?" means trouble is coming, "!!" means they're watching. Hop off the furniture and act like a normal cat
3. **Knock everything off to clear the stage.** Morning, noon, evening and night make one day. How many days can you last?

Walking, jumping, meowing and eating are all "normal cat things", so you won't get in trouble for them. You only get in trouble when you're **caught in the act**.

## 🎮 Controls

| Action | Phone | PC |
|---|---|---|
| Walk | ＜ ＞ at the bottom | ← → / A D |
| Jump | Jump | ↑ / W |
| Punch (hold for a power punch) | Punch | Space / Z |
| Meow | Meow | X |
| Pause | ⏸ top right | Esc / P |

Gamepads work on PC too (D-pad/stick move, A jump, X or B punch, Y meow, START pause). Volume, vibration, mirrored buttons and reduced effects (no screen shake, zoom or flashing) are under **Settings**.

Want to play one-handed? Turn on **One-hand** under **Settings**. Tap a spot or an item and the cat **finds its own way there, jumping as needed**, then punches the item (long tap for a power punch). Tap the boss cat, the chicken, a mouse or the fish tank to paw at it. Swipe up to jump; pressing any button takes back manual control.

## 💡 Tips

- **Strike between patrols.** Stomping footsteps mean the owner is coming back
- **Distract with a meow.** The owner looks where the cat is facing. Get up to mischief on the other side
- **Wait until the owner is busy.** While they're on the phone, cooking, reading or brushing their teeth, they barely notice anything
- **Stare at the camera, then knock it off.** Stand still next to an item and the cat stares at you. Punch now for a "Guilty!" bonus
- **Keep knocking things off unseen for an "Unseen" streak bonus.** The bonus grows with every item
- **Don't give up when you're spotted.** Once per stage, dive into the cardboard box, onto the cat tower, or up on top of the bookshelf or higher within 3 seconds to escape
- **Sparkles, arrows or claw marks?** Something is there. There are no written instructions. Go and find out

- **Cat-meme moments included.** Break something pricey and the cat hops for joy; a big chain makes it stand up and dance. Watch its face when it gets caught (five different reactions). Stand still for a bit and it starts vibing to the music
- **Grab the red charm if you spot one.** It turns up on a shelf now and then. Holding it lets you continue the stage once after you get caught
- **Books, pencils, cans and teddy bears are free-for-all clutter.** Knock them to the floor for "Mess" damage. The owner crouches to tidy up, and only looks at their feet while doing it
- **Mouse on the loose? Bop it.** Mice pop out of a hole now and then and dash around the floor. Bop one for extra time. Chase it toward the owner and they freeze with a scream (your chance!)
- **Don't let the boss cat win.** It knocks things off first and steals the damage. Land 3 paw hits and it runs away. If the owner catches it, it gets scolded and leaves
- **You can walk in front of the bookshelf and the fridge.** The cat tower stands in front of the bookshelf
- **Rub against the owner's legs.** Stay still at their feet to lower suspicion and stop them in their tracks
- **Step on the remote** to turn on the TV. The owner (and the guest) can't look away
- **Yank the tablecloth** hanging off the right end of the table and everything on it slides off
- **Swing on the ceiling lamp.** Leap at it from the hanging shelf, pump left and right, and jump to let go
- **Pile things at one end of the shelf, then stand there…** CLUNK! The shelf tips and dumps everything
- **Creaky boards:** running makes them creak. Hold punch to tiptoe silently
- **From day 2, watch for traps** (noisy foil, sticky tape). From day 3, a pet cam sweeps the room
- **Cucumbers** make the cat leap straight up. Use them as a springboard
- **Churu!** When the owner shakes a churu, the cat gets pulled in. Eat it for time, or mash buttons to resist and leave the owner sulking
- **Hungry? Meow.** A cute meow next to the owner might get you food (= more time)

## 🏠 A day in the house

A day is four stages: morning, noon and evening in rooms that change each day, then a **special night**. Clear the third night to see the ending, then keep going into day 4 and beyond.

| Day | Morning | Noon | Evening | Special night |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Living room | Kitchen | Study | **Dinner guest night** (two watchers, lots of glasses) |
| 2 | Garden | Kid's room | Bathroom | **Blackout night** (pitch dark; only the flashlight beam can spot you) |
| 3 | Living room | Kitchen | Study | **Pet cam night** (two sweeping cameras and traps) |

Each room has its own watcher: the owner, a kid (fast but short-sighted), grandma (slow, but glances back while walking) or dad (keeps looking up at the shelves). The weather changes every day too; on rainy days the owner stays in and comes back sooner.

## 🏆 Rankings and titles

- **World ranking (All-time)**: register a nickname on the result screen to join the top 50 of all players (one entry per device). A record is sent only when you beat your personal best, and a new nickname appears the next time you do
- **Today's ranking**: a ranking for today only (Japan time). Each run is sent to both All-time and Today whenever it beats your best there
- **Send on LINE**: sends the text and the link (the image share sends only the picture on LINE)
- **Records**: lifetime stats from the title screen
- **This device**: your own top 10
- **Titles**: 40 of them. The Titles list shows how to earn even the ones you don't have yet. Collecting them unlocks **accessories** like a bell collar, a ribbon and a crown
- **Crime scene photo**: the moment you're caught becomes a Polaroid, along with your priciest break, your caught face and your best chain. Share it with `#MischievousCat`
- **Bad records**: unsigned records and impossible scores don't appear in the ranking

## ❓ FAQ

- **Does it cost anything?** No. There are no ads either
- **No sound?** Check the speaker button at the top right. On iPhone, silent mode can mute it
- **I updated, but it looks the same.** Close it and open it again (an older version may still be saved for offline play)
- **What about my data?** Your records stay on your device. Only when you join the world ranking are your nickname and record sent
